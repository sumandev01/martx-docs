Chapter 10: Couriers & Shipping Cost Management

Universal courier fleet orchestration, official online tracking portals, zone-based delivery pricing rules, and state-level automated checkout calculations.

Universal Tracking Support

Connect any domestic or international courier service (Sundarban, Steadfast, Pathao, FedEx, UPS) without complex API keys.

Zone Pricing Matrix

Configure granular delivery fees grouped by country, division, and state for automatic checkout rate calculations.

One-Click Buyer Tracking

Customers receive tracking codes and direct portal buttons to query live transit status directly on the courier's website.

Essential Store Requirement — Configuring Shipping Charges is Mandatory:

Before launching your store or accepting live customer orders, administrators must establish active shipping cost rules under Shipping Costs. The MartX storefront checkout engine dynamically reads these geographic zone rules to calculate customer delivery charges. Without configured shipping zones, orders cannot automatically compute accurate delivery tariffs at checkout.

Module Architecture & Administrative Workflow

Shipping and parcel fulfillment in MartX is architected across two synergistic submodules accessible directly under the Ecommerce & Sales sidebar group:

1. Couriers Management

Manages your logistics delivery fleet. Stores carrier names and their public tracking search portal URLs so staff can assign consignment numbers during order fulfillment.

Admin Dashboard > Ecommerce & Sales > Couriers
2. Shipping Costs & Zones

Defines delivery fee rules based on geographic boundaries (e.g., Inside Dhaka, Outside Dhaka, Sub-Urban). Automatically updates order totals during storefront checkout.

Admin Dashboard > Ecommerce & Sales > Shipping Costs

Shipping Zone Coverage & Operational Guidelines (Under-Table Intelligence)

Located directly beneath the shipping rules table, this persistent operational card reinforces critical system constraints for store managers:

Exclusive Location Allocation Each district or state can belong to only one shipping zone. Once a state is mapped, it is automatically excluded from other zone lists to prevent dual-pricing collisions.
Minimum Location Requirement Every active zone must have at least one state selected. The system prevents creating or saving zones with empty location sets.
Reassigning a Location To migrate a state between zones, edit the current zone, uncheck the state, and save. The released state immediately becomes eligible for selection in other zones.
Storefront Real-Time Calculation Selecting a shipping state on the checkout page recalculates the delivery tariff in real time without refreshing the page.

Automated Checkout Calculation Engine
Real-Time

When buyers proceed to the storefront checkout (/checkout), MartX calculates shipping automatically based on their destination address and total cart quantity:

Cart Items Subtotal (8 Items): $408.00
Coupon Discount: $0.00
Shipping Delivery Fee (Standard Worldwide): $38.00 ($10 Base + $28 for 7 extra items)
Calculated Grand Total: $446.00

Anti-Tampering Server Security: The calculation is executed dynamically in the buyer's browser via AJAX and re-computed authoritatively on the backend by OrderRepository. Inspect-element alterations are strictly ignored.

Zero-State Validation Enforcement: Submitting a zone without selecting at least one state will trigger an immediate validation warning: "Validation Error: Please select at least one state or district for this shipping zone." The interface will smoothly scroll to the selector, preventing accidental creation of orphaned or unreachable delivery zones.

10.6 Standard Operating Procedure (SOP): End-to-End Shipping & Logistics Protocol

To ensure error-free parcel handling, tracking transparency, and accurate financial accounting, follow this standardized 5-step operational protocol:

1
Mandatory Setup: Establish Geographic Shipping Zones

Navigate to Shipping Costs > Add New Shipping Cost. Create your operational delivery zones (e.g., Inside City, Outside City, Express) and map all covered states or districts with their respective delivery fees.

2
Register Official Courier Fleet Partners

Navigate to Couriers > Add Courier. Input the carrier name (e.g., Sundarban, Steadfast, Pathao, FedEx) and their public parcel tracking search portal URL.

3
Customer Order Placement & Dynamic Fee Application

The buyer selects their delivery district during checkout. MartX matches the state against configured shipping zones and attaches the exact delivery charge to the order invoice.

4
Warehouse Dispatch & Consignment Tracking Assignment

Once the parcel is packed and handed over to the courier, the warehouse admin opens Edit Order Status, selects the courier partner, inputs the booking consignment code, and marks status as Shipped.

5
Buyer Self-Service Tracking & Delivery Reconciliation

The buyer accesses their order tracking page, copies their consignment code, and clicks "Track on Courier Website" to monitor live transit progress until final delivery confirmation.
