Publishing a comprehensive e-commerce product requires synchronizing commercial copywriting, SKU identification, pricing schedules, multi-level variants, WYSIWYG specifications, image galleries, and SEO metadata. The MartX Add New Product Canvas arranges these controls into a high-productivity dual-column workspace.

1-Click Google Gemini AI Product Copywriter

Manually writing compelling e-commerce product copy, technical bullet points, and Google SEO tags for dozens of SKUs can take hours. MartX integrates Google Gemini 1.5 Flash directly into the master product canvas:

  • The Pro Tip Workflow: Select your Category, Brand, Variants (Size/Color), and Tags first in the right column, then type your Product Name.
  • 1-Click AI Trigger: Click the Auto Generate with AI button with the sparkles icon.
  • Instant 6-Field Synthesis: In under 3 seconds, Gemini synthesizes an engaging Short Description, a rich HTML Full Description with bullet points, structured Product Specifications, an optimized Meta Title (< 60 chars), commercial Meta Keywords, and a high-CTR Meta Description (< 160 chars).
Master Canvas Visual Architecture: 2-Column Responsive Layout MartX organizes the product creation workflow into a synchronized 70% / 30% dual-column interface. The Left Column houses core commercial data, automated SKU creation, time-based promotional calendars, the multi-attribute variation grid, rich WYSIWYG text editors, media gallery dropzones, and Google SERP tags. The Right Column maintains store organization (Category, Subcategory, Brand, Product Status), promotional hashtag badges, the primary square product thumbnail, and the sticky Add New Product publishing trigger.

Enterprise Product Publishing Protocol (Step-by-Step Workflow)

Follow this standardized 7-step protocol to guarantee accurate inventory records, complete SEO indexing, and beautiful storefront presentation:

1 Classification First

Navigate to the Right Column first. Select your Category, Subcategory, and Brand. This establishes the context Gemini AI needs to draft relevant copy.

2 Name & SKU Minting

Type your commercial Product Name in the left column. Click the purple refresh button next to Product SKU to instantly generate a guaranteed unique SKU code.

3 Pricing & Schedules

Enter the Regular Price ($). If hosting a promotion, specify the Discount Price ($) along with optional Start Date & Time and End Date & Time calendars.

4 Configure Variations

If offering sizes or colors, click + Add Variant. Choose size, color, physical stock, and specific photo. Master quantity locks automatically.

5 1-Click AI Copywriting

Click ✨ Auto Generate with AI. In seconds, Gemini populates the Short Description, Full Description, Specs, and 3 SERP SEO fields with commercial copy.

6 Media Attachments

Upload the primary 1:1 Product Main Image in the right column. In the left column, click the gallery dropzone to attach up to 10 additional perspective photos.

7 Step 7: Verification & Final Publication — Ensure Product Status is set to Active and click the purple Add New Product button. Your item is immediately live!

8.3 Multi-Attribute Variations & Attribute Pricing Matrix

Modern fashion, jewelry, and electronics retailing requires items to be offered in multiple sizes, colors, and dimensional configurations. MartX integrates Chapter 11's standardized Sizes and Color Swatches directly into a dynamic, in-canvas Product Variations Matrix.

Variant Configuration Parameters
Variant Field Input Source Storefront Functionality Inheritance Behavior
Size Attribute Dropdown populated from Chapter 11 Size Registry (e.g., XXL, XL, L, M, S). Renders as clickable size selector buttons on the product detail page. Required per variant row.
Color Swatch Dropdown populated from Chapter 11 Color Registry with true HEX codes. Renders as circular visual color swatch pills with active border selection. Required per variant row.
Variant Stock Integer numeric input field. Tracks real-time inventory per specific SKU combination. Prevents over-selling. Sum of variant stocks equals master product quantity.
Variant Image Direct file upload or Central Media Modal selector. When a shopper clicks this color/size, the product main photo switches dynamically. Falls back to master featured image if unassigned.
Custom Price & Schedule Numeric price input and datetime range pickers. Allows premium variations (e.g. larger sizes or special materials) to cost more. Check "Default Main Price" to inherit master pricing.
How to Configure Multi-Attribute Variations (Step-by-Step)
Step 1: Click + Add Variant Inside the Product Variants card, click the purple + Add Variant button to append a new variation row.
Step 2: Assign Size & Color Select the target Size (e.g. "L") and the target Color (e.g. "Purple") from the Chapter 11 attribute rosters.
Step 3: Allocate Stock & Photo Enter the stock count for this SKU (e.g. 20). Click Choose to attach a dedicated photo showing the product in that specific color.
Step 4: Pricing Inheritance Leave Default Main Price checked to inherit the parent price, or uncheck it to enter a custom price and promotional discount schedule.

How to Edit an Existing Product & Calibrate Stock (Step-by-Step Workflow)
Step 1: Open Product Editor From the Product Directory table, click the three-dots action menu next to any SKU and select Edit Product. All data, images, variants, and SEO tags pre-load automatically into the workspace.
Step 2: Adjust Stock & Media Update variant quantities (e.g. increase L/Purple from 20 to 30). Hover over existing gallery images and click the red × badge to prune obsolete angles, or click the dropzone to add new photos.

8.6 Product Merchandising & Catalog Hygiene Best Practices

To maximize sales conversion rates, minimize buyer returns, and achieve dominant Google SERP rankings across competitive product queries, adhere to these enterprise merchandising standards:

Standardized 1:1 Aspect Ratio

Always upload primary product thumbnails in a clean 1:1 square ratio (e.g. 1000 × 1000 px) on neutral backgrounds to maintain grid uniformity across mobile layouts.

Variant Stock Reconciliation

Ensure the sum of individual variant stocks (e.g. 20 on Purple + 21 on Orange = 41) exactly reconciles with the master product quantity to eliminate inventory discrepancy alerts.

Scheduled Flash Sales

Always assign both Start Date and End Date to discount campaigns. Once the end date elapses, MartX automatically reverts pricing back to regular MSRP without manual intervention.

SERP Length Compliance

Keep Meta Titles strictly under 60 characters and Meta Descriptions under 160 characters. Inspect the Search Engine Preview before publishing to ensure zero snippet truncation.
