9.2 Edit Order Status, Payment Transition & Courier Tracking

The Edit Order Status workspace provides warehouse fulfillment teams with a focused, streamlined control panel to advance order lifecycle statuses, record payment settlements, assign third-party courier dispatchers, and input tracking numbers.

Admin Panel — Orders: Edit Order Status & Courier Dispatch Information
Edit Order Status & Tracking Assignment (images/orders/admin_order_status_edit.png)
Interactive Full-Screen Lightbox enabled. Click image or "Full View" to zoom. Order Transition Engine
Update Status & Courier Information

Update to Paid, Pending, or Failed. Marking an unpaid COD order as Paid automatically updates your financial telemetry and revenue ledger.

Select active fulfillment milestone: Pending → Processing → Shipped → Delivered → Canceled. Moving to Delivered sends an automated completion email to the buyer.

Automated Stock & Coupon Recovery: Transitioning an order status to Canceled triggers an atomic database transaction that instantly restores the exact variant quantities (size/color) back to your product inventory ledger and decrements coupon usage count, completely eliminating stock discrepancies without manual inventory adjustments.

Select the courier partner assigned to transport the parcel. This dropdown dynamically populates all active courier services configured in Chapter 10: Couriers & Shipping Costs (e.g., Sundarban Courier, Steadfast, Pathao, SA Paribahan, RedX, FedEx, DHL).

Enter the official courier parcel booking consignment code or memo number (e.g., SB-8921004).

How Customers Track Their Parcel: The tracking number is not auto-appended to the URL. The buyer receives the tracking code directly on their customer order dashboard and invoice, accompanied by a "Track on Courier Website" button. Clicking this button opens the courier's official tracking portal in a new tab, where the customer pastes the consignment number to verify live transit progress.

9.5 Standard Operating Procedure: 5-Step Fulfillment Protocol

Follow this standardized workflow to process, fulfill, and dispatch orders with maximum operational accuracy:

1
Identify & Filter Incoming Orders

Navigate to Ecommerce & Sales > Orders. Click the Pending Orders KPI card or filter by status=pending to isolate new purchases requiring processing.

2
Verify Payment & Stock Availability

Click View Order. Inspect the itemized table to verify product variations, sizes, and colors. For COD orders, verify telephone contact with the buyer before dispatch.

3
Print Warehouse Packaging Slip

Click Print Invoice. Print a thermal packing slip or save as PDF to accompany the parcel in the fulfillment box.

4
Assign Courier & Tracking Code

Navigate to Edit Order Status. Select your configured courier partner (e.g., Sundarban Courier, Steadfast, Pathao, FedEx), enter the parcel consignment tracking number, update order status to Shipped, and click Update Order Status. The customer receives the tracking code with a direct tracking portal link.

5
Final Delivery Reconciliation

Upon courier delivery confirmation, update the order status to Delivered and reconcile the payment status to Paid. Telemetry metrics update instantaneously.
