Automate long-form article drafting, headings, practical tips, and SEO meta tags in under 3 seconds using the integrated AI assistant.

Quill WYSIWYG Editor

Full-featured rich text authoring with video embeds, media manager pictures, bullet formatting, quotes, and 20,000-character capacity.

Module Architecture & Workflow

Dynamic Inactive Warning Safety Mechanism

When an administrator switches a category status to Inactive (either in the Add Form or the Edit Modal), MartX immediately reveals a contextual warning banner:

  • Public Navigation Concealment: The category is immediately removed from storefront top navigation bars, footer links, and sidebar category widgets.
  • Storefront Filtering: Associated articles will not be served on public category browsing pages.
  • Draft Safety: Existing article associations and database relations remain 100% intact and will immediately restore upon reactivating the category.

17.3 Article Creation & Gemini AI Content Studio

Creating high-quality content at scale is accelerated by the MartX Gemini AI Content Studio. By analyzing your chosen blog category and article headline, the AI engine drafts a publication-ready article and complete SEO metadata in a single click.

Admin Panel — Add New Blog: Integrated Gemini AI Content Assistant & Quill WYSIWYG
AI Studio
Core Innovation
Gemini AI Studio: Architecture & Execution Pipeline

The AI Studio container sits directly atop the Blog Content card. It continuously observes form inputs and offers intelligent state guidance:

Pro Tip Optimization

The studio displays the guideline: "Select Category & enter Blog Title for 95%+ accurate AI Article & SEO generation." Supplying category context ensures the AI writes with specific industry vocabulary.

Smart Tooltip & Key Validation

If the GEMINI_API_KEY is missing in System Settings, the button is disabled with tooltip: ⚠️ Please configure Gemini API Key in Settings first. When the title has fewer than 3 characters, the button remains locked with a gentle prompt.

What Happens When You Click "Auto Generate with AI":
  1. Asynchronous API Dispatch: A secure AJAX request is transmitted to admin.ai.generate-content with the title, category context, and type: 'blog'.
  2. Contextual Prompt Engineering: The backend formats a role-based prompt directing Gemini to act as an Elite E-Commerce Content Marketer & SEO Journalist for the current store name.
  3. Structured JSON Response: Gemini returns a clean payload containing:
    • content: Comprehensive HTML article with an engaging hook, "Why This Matters" section, practical steps/tips with ordered lists, and an inspiring conclusion with a subtle store CTA.
    • meta_title: High-CTR SEO headline under 70 characters.
    • meta_keyword: 6 to 8 comma-separated search tags.
    • meta_description: Compelling search snippet under 160 characters.
  4. Instant Form Synchronization: The Quill WYSIWYG editor, hidden form inputs, and all 3 SEO textareas are populated automatically with updated live character count meters.

17.4 Article Revision, Rich Media & Publishing Lifecycle

Editorial content requires ongoing revisions, image refreshes, and seasonal updates. The Edit Blog interface retains existing content while allowing continuous regeneration via AI or manual copy editing.

Admin Panel — Edit Blog: Live Content Revision, Media Thumbnail & SEO Counters
Content Revision
Featured Image Curation

The Featured Image picker interfaces directly with Chapter 6: Media Manager. Administrators can select existing WebP thumbnails or upload fresh high-resolution artwork.

  • Optimized aspect ratio: 5:3 (e.g., 1000px × 600px).
  • Supports WebP, PNG, JPG formats with automated multi-size thumbnail generation.
Inactive Category Protection

If an article's assigned category is later marked inactive, the edit screen alerts the editor:

Previously assigned category is inactive

The administrator can simply reassign the article to another active category to restore full storefront visibility.

What Occurs When Setting a Blog to Inactive:
  • Instant Unpublish: The post is immediately removed from the storefront blog index, recent post carousels, and category archives.
  • Direct URL Protection: Public visitors attempting to visit the slug directly will receive a 404 Not Found error.
  • Draft Preservation: All rich-text markup, user comments, views metrics, and SEO metadata are securely preserved in the database for future republication.

17.6 Content Marketing & Editorial Publishing SOP

To ensure high editorial standards, search visibility, and maximum buyer conversions, marketing teams should execute this 6-step publishing SOP for every new story:

Step 1: Topic & Category Selection

Navigate to Add New Blog and select an active category from the right sidebar dropdown to anchor article taxonomy.

Step 2: Headline Drafting

Input a compelling, search-intent driven headline in Blog Title (e.g., Top 10 Men's Summer Fashion Trends in 2026).

Step 3: AI Generation Trigger

Click ✨ Auto Generate with AI to generate the full article, structured headings, tips, and SEO meta tags.

Step 4: Editorial Refinement

Review the generated text in the Quill WYSIWYG editor. Add store product hyperlinks, brand voice touches, or video embeds.

Step 5: Featured Artwork Assignment

Click Select Image to choose an eye-catching 5:3 aspect ratio cover photo from the MartX Media Manager.

Step 6: Publish & Verify

Ensure Status is set to Active and click Save & Publish Blog. Verify immediate appearance on your public blog storefront.
