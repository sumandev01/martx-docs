12.1 Gateway Selection Console & Architectural Overview

MartX provides a unified, enterprise-grade payment processing architecture supporting 10 distinct gateway integrations across credit/debit cards, digital wallets, global escrow, cryptocurrency rails, and localized offline payments. Administrators manage all payment channels through the centralized Gateway Settings console (Ecommerce & Sales > Payment Gateways > Gateway Settings).

Global Cards

Stripe, PayPal, Razorpay, Paystack, WayForPay

Local & Wallets

SSLCommerz (bKash, Nagad, Rocket), UPI, Netbanking

Crypto Rails

Binance Pay (USDT) & CoinGate (70+ Cryptos)

COD & Offline

Cash on Delivery & Direct Bank / Manual Transfer

Multi-Currency Exchange Rate Engine:

When your storefront operates in a regional base currency (such as BDT, INR, or NGN) while utilizing international gateways that settle in USD or EUR (such as PayPal, WayForPay, or CoinGate), the administrative console provides a dedicated Exchange Rate Field. The checkout engine multiplies order subtotals by this multiplier in real time, ensuring accurate international billing without discrepancy.

12.5 Cash on Delivery (COD) & Advance Shipping Charge Enforcement

Cash on Delivery is a vital payment method in emerging markets but carries high return and refusal risks (RTO). MartX features a sophisticated Advance Delivery Charge Architecture that eliminates fake orders by requiring buyers to pay the courier shipping fee online before confirming their COD shipment.

Admin Panel — Cash On Delivery: Advance Shipping Charge & Mandatory Method Selection
Cash On Delivery Configuration Interface (images/payments/admin_gateway_cod.png)
Live Screenshot: Red warning banner enforcing minimum 1 active advance payment method. Advance COD Security
Mandatory Selection Validation Architecture
1. Client-Side Instant Prevention

When "Require Advance Shipping Charge?" is toggled ON, the system inspects the Allowed Advance Payment Methods checkboxes. If an administrator clicks "Save And Update" without selecting at least one active gateway, submission is halted instantly (e.preventDefault()), the screen scrolls smoothly to the container, and an inline red warning is revealed: "Selection Required: Please select at least one active payment gateway to collect advance shipping charges."

2. Server-Side Backend Safeguard

The backend controller (PaymentMethodController::updateGateways) strictly validates that when payment_cod_advance_shipping == '1', the payment_cod_advance_methods payload must not be empty. Any malformed request is rejected with a session flash error, completely preventing database corruption or headless checkout deadlocks.

12.8 Payment Settlement, Webhooks & Security SOP

Adhere to enterprise financial engineering best practices when deploying live payment channels on MartX:

Security & Key Management
  • Encrypted Credential Storage: All Secret Keys, Auth Tokens, and Passwords are automatically AES-256 encrypted in the database before storage.
  • Sandbox Verification: Always run complete checkout transactions using Sandbox test cards before flipping environment toggles to Live Mode.
  • Webhook URL Whitelisting: Configure your gateway portal webhooks to point to your store's secure SSL endpoint to guarantee instant payment state callbacks.
Financial Accounting & Conversion
  • Never Enter 0 for Exchange Rates: Setting an exchange rate to 0 causes payment calculation failure. If your store currency matches the gateway, set it to 1.00.
  • Mitigate COD Abandonment: Keep Require Advance Shipping Charge? active to ensure customer commitment and protect logistics capital.
  • Footer Trust Badges: Keep 4–6 high-resolution payment logos in the visual logos hub to optimize buyer checkout conversion.
