16.1 Dynamic Custom Pages & Policy Manager

Every modern enterprise e-commerce platform requires legal compliance and institutional transparency. The Dynamic Pages Manager allows administrators to publish and maintain legal policies (Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy, Shipping & Return Guarantees) alongside custom promotional landing pages with automatic SEO slug routing and instant live status toggles.

Admin Panel — Dynamic Pages Management Table
Pages Registry
Dynamic Pages Table (images/cms/admin_pages_table.png)
Centralized registry displaying all active policies, dynamic slugs, and quick management actions. MartX v1.0.0
Admin Access Route

Navigate to: Admin Dashboard → Website Setup → Pages. From here, click + Create Page to launch the authoring canvas.

Pages Registry Anatomy & Field Specifications
Column / Attribute Admin Controls & Data Type System Purpose & Architecture Storefront Impact
SL / Serial Sequential ID Auto-incremented listing order for fast identification in the management grid. Internal administrative reference only.
Title Text String (e.g., "Terms and Conditions") The primary human-readable title of the document. Displays prominently at the top of the rendered page. Rendered as the primary <h1> page header and default HTML title tag.
Slug URL Slug (e.g., terms-and-conditions) Unique URL-encoded identifier generated automatically from the title or customized by the admin. Resolves public access via clean routing: https://yourstore.com/page/{slug}.
Status Active / Inactive Switch Instant AJAX state controller. Setting to Inactive takes the page offline immediately without deletion. When Inactive, the public URL returns a clean 404 response and removes links from footers.
Actions Edit / Delete Buttons Launches the full-page editing suite or prompts a secure two-step modal deletion confirmation. Edits update live storefront content immediately upon saving.

16.2 Creating & Editing Pages with Google Gemini AI

Drafting comprehensive e-commerce policies like Privacy Policies or Return Guarantees typically requires hours of legal drafting. MartX features a built-in 1-Click Google Gemini AI Policy Generator directly integrated into the page authoring canvas. With a single click, store information is synthesized into enterprise-grade legal prose.

Admin Panel — Create Dynamic Page with Gemini AI Assistant
AI Policy Generator
Add Page with Gemini AI (images/cms/admin_page_add.png)
Notice the prominent "Auto Generate with AI" button, Summernote WYSIWYG editor with live character counter, and SEO fields. MartX v1.0.0
How 1-Click AI Policy Generation Operates

When an administrator enters a page title (e.g. "Privacy Policy", "Return and Refund Policy", or "Shipping Terms") and clicks Auto Generate with AI, MartX executes a background API prompt utilizing Google Gemini 1.5 Flash:

1. Store Context Injection Pulls Store Name, Support Email, Hotline Phone, and Physical Address directly from Chapter 5 General Settings.
2. Legal Synthesis Generates formatted HTML clauses (Data Collection, Cookies, Return Windows, Chargebacks) structured with headings and bullet points.
3. SERP SEO Automation Simultaneously writes optimized Meta Titles (under 70 chars), comma-separated Keywords, and Meta Descriptions (under 160 chars).

Live view of registered legal clauses with real-time character tracker (2,513 / 20,000 chars) and pre-filled SEO metadata. MartX v1.0.0

16.3 Multi-Block About Us Page Builder

Rather than requiring store owners to construct an About Us page using raw HTML, MartX provides a dedicated, multi-block About Us Page Builder. This structured interface breaks the public /about-us storefront page into cohesive brand storytelling components: Hero Storytelling, Strategic Pillars, and a dynamic Call-to-Action (CTA) showroom card.

Admin Panel — Multi-Block About Us Page Builder
Page Builder
About Us Page Builder (images/cms/admin_about_page.png)
Multi-section builder configuring General Brand Story, Mission & Vision statements, and Showroom CTA media. MartX v1.0.0
Admin Access Route

Navigate to: Admin Dashboard → Website Setup → About Us Page. Click Save Changes to publish updates directly to /about-us.

Block 1
General Brand Story

Defines the hero introduction at the top of the About page:

  • Page Title: Hero headline (e.g., "Crafting Extraordinary Shopping Experiences").
  • Subtitle: Supporting brand ethos statement.
  • About Content: In-depth brand journey, founding philosophy, and craftsmanship narrative.
Block 2
Strategic Pillars

Structured cards reinforcing enterprise trust:

  • Our Mission: Clear actionable commitment to customer satisfaction and quality.
  • Our Vision: Forward-looking aspirational statement for brand leadership.
  • Core Values: Integrity, customer obsession, and sustainable sourcing standards.
Block 3
Media CTA Card

High-converting promotional callout card:

  • Showroom Image: High-res photo uploaded via MartX Media Modal.
  • Button Text: Actionable label (e.g., "Explore Catalog").
  • Target Link: Destination routing to store categories, collections, or custom campaigns.
Public Team Members & Leadership Showcase: To manage executive portraits, designations, social links, and drag-and-drop hierarchy for the public About Us page, navigate to Admin Dashboard → Administration & Settings → Team Members. 16.5 CMS Best Practices & SEO Guidelines

To ensure high search visibility, rapid page load speeds, and legal compliance across multiple international jurisdictions, follow these core architectural guidelines when managing MartX CMS assets:

SERP Strict Limits

Keep Meta Titles strictly under 70 characters and Meta Descriptions under 160 characters. MartX real-time counters ensure zero truncation on Google search snippets.

Mandatory Legal Quartet

Every online storefront should maintain at minimum: Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy, Shipping Policy, and Return & Refund Policy to meet credit card processor requirements.

Optimized Visual Media

Upload optimized WebP or JPG/PNG image assets with consistent aspect ratios for About Us company stories and custom policy pages. This guarantees lightning-fast loading across all devices.

Order Hierarchy

Place high-volume queries (e.g., order tracking and return methods) at the top of the FAQ list using Sortable.js drag handles to resolve customer questions before they file support tickets.
