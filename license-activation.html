Chapter 4: License Activation, Grace Period & Domain Migration

Welcome to the official MartX License Activation & Verification Hub. Designed to deliver a smooth, frictionless merchant experience, MartX provides automated cloud verification, an initial 7-day live trial window, free local development testing, and an instant 1-click license key re-binding workflow when migrating to a new domain.

4.3 7-Day Live Domain Installation Trial Window

We understand that merchants and agencies often need time to connect their payment gateways, upload catalog items, and verify storefront operations on a live production domain before permanently binding their license key.

Note: This 7-day trial applies only to new, unactivated live installations. It is distinct from the 14-day offline fail-safe grace period described in Section 4.7.

How the 7-Day Window Works for Buyers
  • Instant Unrestricted Access: From the moment you first install MartX on any live domain (e.g., yourdomain.com), you receive a full 7-Day Free Trial Window with 100% access to all admin features.
  • Dynamic Dual-State Warning Banner: When trial days exceed 2 days, a warm Amber Banner displays remaining time (⏳ X Days Remaining). On the final 48 hours, the banner dynamically switches to an urgent Crimson Emergency Banner (⚠️ Urgent: Only X Hours Left - Action Required) ensuring you have ample warning.
  • Zero Customer Interruption: Even while unactivated during the grace period, your public storefront, product catalogs, and shopping cart checkout remain 100% active and operational.
  • Server Clock Consistency Protection: To prevent accidental system clock rollback or date manipulation from disrupting your trial calculations, the system verifies server time consistency against the initial installation timestamp and database records.

4.4 Admin Panel Lockdown Screen & How to Unlock

If the initial 7-day live trial window elapses without entering a valid purchase code, your Administrative Panel safely enters the License Verification Lockdown Screen.

In Lockdown Mode, standard administrative routes are paused until the license is activated. Your store's data (products, orders, customers) remains completely safe and intact in your database; however, customer-facing catalog browsing and checkout are temporarily paused until a valid license is verified.

4.6 License Key Re-binding & Migration to a New Domain

Under official commercial licensing, a single MartX purchase code can be actively bound to one live production domain at a time. However, if you are migrating from a development/staging subdomain to your primary domain (e.g., from staging.mystore.com to mystore.com) or rebranding your business under a new domain name, you do not need to purchase a new license key.

MartX includes an automated 1-Click License Relocation & Re-binding workflow. You can seamlessly move your existing license key to your new domain without contacting support or editing database tables:

Step 1: Request Key Relocation
Trigger License Re-binding

On your new live domain's admin panel (or the locked screen), click "Transfer License to This Domain" and enter your existing Purchase Code along with your registered Buyer Email address.

Step 2: Instant Email Authorization
1-Click Secure Activation

Check your buyer email inbox. You will receive an automated, secure 10-minute confirmation link. Simply click "Confirm Domain Transfer" — your purchase code is instantly released from the old domain and locked to your new live store with zero downtime.

4.7 14-Day Offline Fail-Safe Protection & Weekly Check-in

Store owners never have to worry about sales interruptions caused by external internet fluctuations, cloud maintenance, or temporary hosting connectivity drops. MartX is engineered with a built-in merchant safety net.

Weekly Admin Check-in (Every 7 Days)

Once activated, the system only checks in with the central license gateway once every 7 days. This verification is triggered exclusively when an administrator accesses the Administrative Dashboard. Customer storefront browsing never makes outbound license calls.

Fast Customer Storefront

When shoppers browse your catalog, add items to cart, and complete orders, MartX verifies authorization from fast local cached files. Customers experience immediate page responses without waiting on external third-party licensing servers.

Continuous Sales Guarantee & Offline Tolerance
  • 14-Day Fail-Safe Window: If your web hosting server cannot reach the cloud licensing gateway during routine check-ins, MartX automatically triggers a 14-Day Fail-Safe Grace Period. Your storefront, catalog, and checkout remain fully open, and customers can shop with zero disruption.
  • Quiet 24-Hour Reconnection Retry: While the connection is unavailable, MartX never overloads your server with constant retries. It checks softly once every 24 hours in the background when an admin visits the panel.
  • Automatic Recovery: As soon as internet connectivity is restored, the next admin visit validates the license and resets the normal 7-day cycle automatically with no manual reconfiguration needed.
  • Extended Outage Protection (> 14 Days): If server disconnection persists past 14 consecutive days, the admin panel safely displays the verification recovery screen. Once internet access returns, clicking "Re-Verify License" instantly restores full access in under 2 seconds.

4.8 In-Settings License Console, Key Switching & Safe Deactivation

Super Administrators can inspect active credentials, switch purchase codes, or relocate licenses directly from the dedicated management console located at:

Super Admin Exclusive Path: Admin Sidebar → System Settings → "License & Verification" (Tab)

The License & Verification console is organized into two operational workstations:

1. Current License Overview & Key Switcher
  • Active Credentials Display: Displays your masked purchase code (e.g., 94b1a45e-****-****-b301), buyer name, marketplace channel badge, bound domain, verification date, and cloud heartbeat health.
  • Switch / Update License Key: An inline collapsible form allows you to replace or upgrade your license key on the fly without wiping store settings or reinstalling the application.
  • Safe Deactivation: Clicking "Deactivate License" triggers a confirmation modal to cleanly unlink the license key from this store so it can be deployed on another project.
2. In-Dashboard Domain Migration Desk
  • Target Domain Input: Moving your store to a new domain or sub-domain? Simply type the destination domain name directly into the migration field.
  • 1-Click Email Dispatch: Clicking "Send 1-Click Transfer Email Link" dispatches an authorization link to your registered buyer email address.
  • Instant Domain Re-binding: Confirming the link via your inbox unlocks the new store domain instantly with zero downtime.

4.9 Merchant Privacy & Zero-Telemetry Architecture

We design the MartX license verification system to strictly minimize data collection in line with international privacy best practices. The verification mechanism is strictly confined to software entitlement: it never monitors, accesses, or exports your private business operations.

What Is Verified During Check-in What Is NEVER Collected or Transmitted
  • Purchase Code / License Key: To confirm purchase authenticity.
  • Domain Address (URL): To bind the license to your active store.
  • Marketplace Identifier: To identify where the item was purchased (e.g., CodeCanyon).
  • Buyer Email Address: Used strictly for 1-click domain transfer authorization.
  • Customer Personal Data: Zero customer names, emails, phone numbers, or passwords.
  • Payment & Banking Details: Zero credit card numbers, bank records, or gateway secrets.
  • Product & Pricing Data: Zero product catalogs, pricing tiers, or inventory quantities.
  • Sales & Revenue Telemetry: Zero order totals, sales amounts, or profit analytics.
100% Data Sovereignty: All of your store's customer records, transactional history, and financial metrics stay strictly on your own database server and are never shared with any external entity.

4.10 Live Production Deployment Checklist & Best Practices

When moving your MartX store from a staging computer to a live web hosting environment, ensure the following quick checks are completed:

1. Set Production Environment

In your server's .env file, set APP_ENV=production and update APP_URL=https://yourdomain.com to match your live store's exact domain. This ensures security protections (such as localhost spoofing prevention) engage properly.

2. Enable Outbound HTTPS (Port 443)

Ensure your hosting provider allows standard outbound HTTPS web traffic on port 443 so your store can contact the global license gateway during activation and weekly checks. Standard cPanel, Plesk, VPS, and Cloud servers have this open by default.

3. Moving Between Domains

A standard commercial license is actively bound to one live domain at a time. If you initially test on a live staging subdomain (e.g., staging.mystore.com) and later launch on mystore.com, use the built-in 1-Click Domain Transfer Desk in Chapter 4.6.
