18.2 Blog Discussion & Comment Moderation Engine

Active blog engagement drives organic search rankings and community trust. The Blog Comments console gives administrators full control over discussion threads with auto-approval safety toggles.

Admin Panel — Blog Comments: Global Auto-Approval Settings & Comment Ledger
Comments Hub
Auto-Approval Control & Anti-Spam Strategy
If enabled, new blog comments will be published immediately without waiting for admin approval.
Security Best Practice: While Auto Approve accelerates community engagement, stores experiencing high bot traffic should leave this switch OFF to review comments manually before public rendering.
Admin Panel — Comment Details Modal: Reader Feedback & Blog Context
Comment Inspector
Comment Modal Parameters:
  • Blog Post: Full title of the target article (e.g., Exclusive Autumn-Winter 2026 Collection Preview).
  • Commenter Name: Author's public display name (e.g., Suman Chandra Dev Sharma).
  • Email Address: Submitter's email for administrative follow-up or reply notifications.
  • Date Posted: Precise timestamp (e.g., Sep 17, 2026, 10:24 AM).
  • Full Comment: Unabridged multi-paragraph reader commentary with preserved whitespace formatting.

18.4 Gemini AI Email Reply Studio & Live HTML Template Preview

MartX pioneers integrated AI customer service. When viewing any contact message, clicking Reply to Message directs the administrator into the Reply Studio, featuring one-click AI draft generation and real-time responsive HTML email previewing.

Admin Panel — Reply to Customer Message: Gemini AI Assistant & Live Branded Email Preview
Gemini AI Studio
AI Customer Specialist
Gemini AI Support Specialist: Workflow Architecture

When an administrator clicks ✨ Auto Generate with AI on the reply screen:

  1. Context Extraction: The customer's full name, email address, original inquiry subject, and inquiry text are packaged and sent to the endpoint admin.contact-message.generate-ai-reply.
  2. Persona-Driven Reasoning: Google Gemini assumes the persona of a Senior Customer Support Specialist for your store brand, generating:
    • An appropriate email subject line prefixed with Re:.
    • A personalized greeting addressing the customer by their first name.
    • An empathetic, polite response directly resolving their inquiry.
    • An official sign-off with the store's customer support signature.
  3. DOM Synchronization: The subject input and message textarea are filled instantly.
  4. Live HTML Template Sync: The right-side Live Email Preview updates in real-time, rendering the exact responsive HTML template the customer will receive in their inbox.
Field / Component Type & Limit Mandatory Operational Functionality & Validation
Email Subject Text Input (Max 255) Required Specifies the outgoing SMTP email subject line. Automatically pre-filled with Re: [Original Subject] or populated by Gemini AI.
Reply Message Body Textarea (9 Rows) Required Main content of the reply email. Supports custom editing, manual refinement, or 100% AI generation.
Live Email Preview Interactive Component Visual Aid Re-renders on every keystroke, showing branded header banner, greeting, body copy, quoted inquiry block, and store footer links.
Send Email Reply Submit Button Action Dispatches email via queued SMTP, marks the ticket status as Replied, and logs the outbound transmission timestamp.

18.6 Customer Interaction & Moderation SOP

To maintain 5-star customer satisfaction and ensure brand integrity, customer success agents should execute this daily 5-step Standard Operating Procedure:

Step 1: Daily Inbox & Review Audit

Review the top-bar notification count for new contact inquiries and check the Pending Approval metric card on Product Reviews.

Step 2: Review Moderation

Inspect star ratings and feedback text in the Review Modal. Approve legitimate buyer feedback or reject spam.

Step 3: Blog Comment Moderation

Audit new comments under Blog Comments. Verify compliance with store community guidelines before approving.

Step 4: AI Reply Generation

Open Unread Contact Messages, click Reply to Message, and use ✨ Auto Generate with AI to draft responses.

Step 5: Preview & Dispatch

Verify the generated response against the Live Email Preview and click Send Email Reply to resolve the ticket.

Step 6: Audience Sync

Perform weekly exports from Newsletters via Export CSV to synchronize marketing subscriber databases.
